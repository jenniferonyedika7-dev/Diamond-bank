# Diamond Bank: Customer User Guide

This guide explains how to use Diamond Bank online banking: registering, signing in, checking your
accounts, sending money, managing your debit cards and taking out a loan.

Words in **bold** are exactly what you see on the screen: page titles, menu items, buttons and messages.

Some rules, such as your daily transfer limit, how long you can stay signed in without doing anything,
and the smallest and largest loan you can ask for, are set by the bank and can change. The screens
always show you the current figures, so this guide does not quote fixed numbers.

## 1. Registering online

Registering online creates your online banking profile and login. Your first bank account is then
opened for you at a branch, after the bank has checked your identity (this check is called **KYC**,
"know your customer").

### Fill in the registration form

1. Go to the **Sign in** page and click **Register online** (under "New customer?").
2. The **Open an account** form appears. All fields are required.
3. Under **Personal details**, enter your **First name**, **Last name**, **Date of birth**, **Gender** and
   **National ID number**. You must be at least 18.
4. Under **Contact**, enter your **Phone number**, **Email address** and choose your **Home branch**.
   - The phone number must be a 9-digit Gambian mobile number starting with 83, 86 or 87, for example
     871234567. Do not add the country code. Spaces are removed for you.
5. Under **Sign-in details**, choose a **Username** and a **Password**, then type the password again in
   **Confirm password**. Your password must have:
   - At least 8 characters
   - At least one letter
   - At least one number
6. Click **Create account**.

If everything is correct, you return to the **Sign in** page with the message **Registration successful.
You can now log in.**

If the form shows **Registration is not open yet. The bank has no branches set up.**, the bank is still
being set up. Please try again later.

### What happens next: identity check and your first account

You can sign in straight away, but you cannot bank online until two things are done at the branch:

1. Your identity is checked. Until then, your pages show **Your identity is being verified. Visit
   (your branch) with your national ID.** Take your national ID to your home branch.
2. Your first account is opened. After your identity is verified, branch staff open your account. Until
   then, your pages show **Visit a branch to open your first account.**

If the bank could not verify you, your pages show **Your verification was not approved. Please contact
the bank.**

## 2. Signing in for the first time

1. Open the **Sign in** page ("Welcome back. Sign in to your Diamond Bank account.").
2. Enter your **Username** and **Password**.
3. Click **Sign in**.

You land on your **Overview** page.

If your branch gave you a temporary password (see [Locked out or blocked](#12-locked-out-or-blocked)),
you are taken to **Change your password** first and see **You must change your password before
continuing.** Choose a new password before you do anything else.

## 3. Finding your way: the top bar and your user menu

Every page has a dark blue bar across the top. Click the **Diamond Bank** logo at any time to go back
to your **Overview**.

### The main menu

| Menu item | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Your total balance, your accounts and your most recent transactions |
| **Accounts** | All your accounts; click one to see its full history |
| **Transfer** | Send money to another Diamond Bank account |
| **Cards** | Request, view and block your debit cards |
| **Loans** | Apply for a loan, follow it and repay it |

The page you are on is underlined in gold.

### Your user menu

On the right of the bar you see your initials and your name, with **Customer** underneath. Click it to
open your user menu:

- **Profile**: the personal details the bank holds for you
- **Change password**
- **Log out**

### On a phone or small screen

The menu folds into a **Menu** button at the top right. Tap it to see the same menu items, your user
menu and **Log out**.

## 4. Overview and your accounts

### Overview

Open **Overview**. You see:

- A greeting (**Hello, (your first name)**)
- Your **Total balance** across all your accounts
- A **Transfer money** button
- **Your accounts**: one tile per account, showing its type, status, balance and account number
- **Recent transactions** across all your accounts

### Accounts

1. Open **Accounts** ("Choose an account to see its full history.").
2. Click an account tile to open that account.

On the account page you see:

- The account type, its status and the account number
- The **Balance**, the account's **Minimum balance**, its interest rate and its branch
- A **Transfer from this account** button (only when the account is active)
- The account's **Transaction history**

Each account has one of these statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Active** | You can use the account normally |
| **Frozen** | The branch has frozen it. No money can go in or out. The page shows **This account is frozen. Please contact your branch.** |
| **Closed** | The account is closed |

## 5. Transaction history and filters

The **Transaction history** on an account page lists every transaction, newest first. For each one
you see the **Date**, **Type**, **Amount** (green with + for money in, red with − for money out), the
**Balance after**, the **Channel** (for example online or at a branch), the **Branch** and the
**Description**.

### Filtering the list

Above the list there are three filters:

1. **Type**: choose **All types**, **Deposits**, **Withdrawals**, **Transfers in**, **Transfers out**,
   **Loan disbursements** (a loan paid into your account) or **Loan payments**.
2. **From**: the first date to show.
3. **To**: the last date to show.

The list updates as soon as you change a filter. To go back to the full list, click **Clear filters**.

If nothing matches, you see **No transactions match these filters.** When the list is long, use the page
buttons under it to move between pages.

## 6. Sending money (transfers)

You can send money from your own active account to any other active Diamond Bank account. A transfer
takes three steps.

### Step 1 of 3: Transfer details

1. Open **Transfer** (or click **Transfer money** on the Overview, or **Transfer from this account** on an
   account page).
2. Under **From account**, choose the account to send from. Frozen or closed accounts are listed but
   cannot be chosen.
   - Under the box you see what you can send today: **Remaining today**, the account's **daily limit**,
     and **You can send up to** an amount that keeps the account's minimum balance.
3. Enter the **Recipient account number** (for example DB0010000002).
4. Enter the **Amount (GMD)**: more than zero, with at most 2 decimal places.
5. Optionally, add a **Description (optional)**.
6. Click **Continue**. The bank looks up the recipient.

### Step 2 of 3: Review and confirm

1. Check the summary: **Send (amount) to (recipient)**. To protect privacy, the recipient's name is
   partly hidden.
2. Check the **From:** account and the **Description:**.
3. Enter **Your password** ("We ask for your password to confirm it's you.").
4. Click **Send money**. To change something, click **Back** instead.

### Step 3 of 3: Result

- If the transfer works, you see **Transfer complete.** with the amount and the recipient, and the **New
  balance** of your account. You can click **View account** or **Make another transfer**.
- If the bank refuses the transfer, you see the reason and **No money was moved.** Click **Back to edit**
  to change the details and try again.

### The daily transfer limit

Each account has a daily transfer limit. The bank sets it for each type of account, so it can
differ between your accounts.

- You can transfer up to your account's daily limit in one day. The day runs from midnight to midnight,
  Banjul time, and the limit resets at midnight.
- All transfers out of the account that day count towards the limit.
- Before you send, the **From account** box shows how much of today's limit is left.
- A transfer that would go over the limit is refused with a message that says the limit and how much is
  left, for example **This transfer is over the daily transfer limit of GMD … for this account. Remaining
  today: GMD ….**

### Why a transfer can be refused

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| **This account can't receive transfers. Check the number and try again.** | The recipient's account number is wrong, or that account can't receive money right now |
| **You can't transfer to the same account.** | The "from" and "to" accounts are the same |
| **Insufficient funds: this transfer would take the account below its minimum balance.** | You don't have enough money; every account has to keep its minimum balance |
| **This transfer is over the daily transfer limit …** | See [The daily transfer limit](#the-daily-transfer-limit) above |
| **The source account is not active.** / **The destination account is not active.** | One of the accounts was frozen or closed |
| **Both accounts must use the same currency.** | The two accounts hold different currencies |
| **The password is incorrect.** | Type your password again. Too many wrong passwords lock you out (see [section 12](#12-locked-out-or-blocked)) |

## 7. Debit cards

Open **Cards** ("Request a debit card for one of your accounts, and block a card if it is lost or
stolen.").

Each card tile shows the card type, its status, the last digits of the card number, the **Account:** it
belongs to, **Expires:** and the card's **Daily limit:**.

| Card status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Requested** | Waiting for your branch to review ("Your branch will review it.") |
| **Active** | The card works |
| **Blocked** | The card has been blocked and no longer works |
| **Rejected** | The branch turned the request down. The tile shows the **Reason:** |
| **Expired** | The card has passed its expiry date |

### Requesting a card

You can have one card per account. Frozen or closed accounts can't have a card.

1. Under **Request a debit card**, choose the **Account**.
2. Choose the **Card type**. Each type shows its daily limit.
3. Click **Request card**.

You see **Card requested. The bank will review your request.** Your branch then issues the card, or
rejects the request and gives a reason.

If every one of your active accounts already has a card or a pending request, you see **Each of your
active accounts already has a card or a pending request.**

### Showing the full card number

For your security, the full number is hidden. To see it:

1. On an **Active** card, click **Show number**.
2. The **Show card number** box appears. Enter your **Password** and click **Show number**. Make sure no
   one is looking at your screen.
3. The full number appears for 30 seconds. You can click **Copy** to copy it, or **Hide** to hide it
   straight away. It also hides itself when you switch to another tab or leave the page.

A wrong password counts towards the lockout described in [section 12](#12-locked-out-or-blocked).

### Blocking a lost or stolen card

1. On an **Active** card, click **Block card**.
2. The **Block this card?** box explains that the card stops working straight away and that only your
   branch can unblock it.
3. Click **Block card** to confirm.

You see **Card blocked. You can request a replacement.** You can then request a new card for the same
account. If you find the old card, ask your branch to unblock it. This is only possible while no
replacement card is active on the account.

## 8. Loans

Open **Loans** ("Apply for a loan and follow it from application to the last repayment.").

You can have one loan at a time. While you have an application in progress or an active loan, you
see **You can have one loan at a time. You can apply again once your current loan is closed, rejected or
cancelled.** and the **Apply for a loan** button is hidden.

### How a loan is decided

Every loan is checked twice:

1. **Your branch** checks your application: they review your bank statement, verify your employment,
   contact your guarantor and verify the guarantor's occupation. They then approve or reject it.
2. **An administrator** gives the final approval. The money is paid into your chosen account at that
   moment, and your repayment schedule starts.

Your loan shows one of these statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Your branch is checking your application |
| **Awaiting admin** | Your branch has approved it and it is waiting for final approval |
| **Active** | The loan has been paid out and you are repaying it |
| **Rejected** | The loan was not approved. You can see the reason |
| **Cancelled** | You cancelled the application |
| **Closed** | The loan is fully repaid |

### Applying for a loan (4 steps)

1. Open **Loans**, then click **Apply for a loan**.

**Step 1 of 4: Loan details**

2. Choose the **Loan type**. Each type shows its yearly interest rate.
3. Under **Pay the loan into**, choose the account that should receive the money. Only your active
   accounts are listed.
4. Enter the **Amount (GMD)**. The hint under the box shows the smallest and largest loan the bank
   currently allows.
5. Choose the **Term** (how many months).
6. Choose a **Repayment plan**:
   - **Monthly instalments**: "You repay every month. Each payment covers that month's interest and part
     of the loan."
   - **Single payment at the end**: "You repay the whole loan and all its interest in one payment at the
     end of the term."
7. Wait a moment for **Your quote**. It shows the **Monthly instalment** (or **Single payment**), the
   **Number of payments**, the **Total interest** and the **Total repayable**.
8. Choose **What is the loan for?** and fill in **Describe the purpose**.
9. Click **Next**.

**Step 2 of 4: Employment and income**

10. Enter your **Monthly income (GMD)** and your **TIN (Taxpayer Identification Number)**: 8 to 15 digits.
11. Choose your **Employment**: **Employed**, **Business owner**, **Content creator** or **Other**, then
    fill in the extra boxes that appear (for example **Employer name** and **Job title**).
12. Check **Your contact details**. If they are wrong, ask your branch to update your profile.
13. Click **Next**.

**Step 3 of 4: Guarantor**

14. Enter your guarantor's **full name**, **phone**, **occupation**, **address** and **email**. The
    bank will contact your guarantor to confirm they agree to stand behind the loan. Your guarantor can't
    be you: their phone number and email must be different from yours.
15. Click **Next**.

**Step 4 of 4: Review and submit**

16. Check each section. Click **Edit** next to a section to go back and change it.
17. Look at the **Repayment schedule (preview)**. The due dates are estimates: the real dates count
    from the day the loan is approved.
18. Tick the box: **I confirm these details are true, and I understand the bank will check them, review
    my bank statement and contact my guarantor before deciding.**
19. Click **Submit application**.

You return to **Loans** with the message **Loan application submitted. The bank will review it.**

### Affordability

Before deciding, the bank compares your monthly repayment with the monthly income you declared. If the
repayment takes a large share of your income (the bank sets the level), the people reviewing your loan
see a warning. The warning does not refuse the loan by itself: they weigh it together with your bank
statement and the other checks. You do not see this warning yourself, so make sure the income you declare
is accurate.

### Cancelling an application

You can cancel while your loan is **Pending** or **Awaiting admin**.

1. On **Loans**, click **Cancel** next to the application (or open it and click **Cancel application**).
2. The **Cancel this application?** box appears. Click **Cancel application** to confirm.

You see **Loan application cancelled.** You can apply again afterwards.

### Your loan page: summary, schedule and payment history

On **Loans**, click **View** next to a loan. The loan page shows:

- **Repayment** (active loans only): the **Outstanding balance**, the **Next payment** and its due date,
  and, for monthly loans, the **Instalments left**
- **Summary**: the loan terms, the account it was **Paid into account**, the **Purpose**, and the dates it
  was **Approved by your branch** and given **Final approval and payout**
- **Repayment schedule**: one line per instalment with its due date, **Principal**, **Interest**,
  **Amount**, **Balance after** and a status: **Paid**, **Next payment**, **Upcoming**, **Overdue** or
  **Settled** (cleared when the loan was paid off early). Before approval you see **Your schedule appears
  here once the loan is approved.**
- **Payment history**: every payment, with the **Date**, **Amount**, **Channel** (online, or cash at a
  branch), what it **Covered** and the **Loan balance after**

If an instalment is late, you see **1 instalment is overdue. Please pay as soon as you can. No penalty
fees are charged.**

When the loan is fully repaid, you see **This loan is paid off … There is nothing more to pay.**

### Paying your loan online

From the loan page, click **Pay next instalment** (monthly loans) or **Pay everything now**. A loan with
a single payment at the end can only be paid with **Pay everything now**.

**Step 1 of 3: What to pay**

1. Under **What do you want to pay?**, choose:
   - **Next instalment**: shows the instalment number, its due date, and its principal and interest.
   - **Pay everything now**: clears the whole loan. Interest you have not yet been charged may be waived;
     the screen shows how much.
2. Choose the **Pay from account**. The hint shows how much is **Available** while keeping the account's
   minimum balance.
3. Click **Continue**.

**Step 2 of 3: Review and confirm**

4. Check **Pay (amount) towards loan #…**, the **Payment:** type and the **From:** account.
5. Enter **Your password** and click **Pay (amount)**.

**Step 3 of 3: Result**

- If the payment works, you see **Payment received. … was paid from account ….** (and **Your loan is now
  paid off.** if it was the last payment), with the account's new balance and what is **Left to pay on the
  loan**.
- If it is refused, you see the reason and **No money was moved.** Click **Try again** or **Back to the
  loan**.

Good to know:

- A loan payment does not count towards your daily transfer limit, but it must leave the account's
  minimum balance in place.
- If the amount due changes while you are paying (for example, an instalment has just become overdue),
  you see **The amount has changed. Please review it again.** together with the new amounts.
  Check them and pay again.
- You can also pay in cash at your branch. Cash payments appear in the payment history with the channel
  **Branch**.

## 10. Changing your password

1. Open your user menu and choose **Change password**.
2. On **Change your password**, enter your **Current password**.
3. Enter a **New password** and type it again in **Confirm new password**. It must have at least 8

4. Click **Change password**.

## 11. "Are you still there?": signing out when idle

To protect your money, you are signed out automatically if you do nothing for a while. The bank sets
how long.

- Shortly before that happens, a box appears: **Are you still there?** It counts down: **You will be
  logged out in … seconds because there has been no activity.**
- Click **Stay signed in** to carry on.
- If you don't, you are signed out and the **Sign in** page shows **You were logged out after … minutes of
  inactivity.** Simply sign in again.

Clicking, typing, scrolling and moving between pages all count as activity. If you have Diamond Bank open
in more than one tab, activity in any of them keeps you signed in.

## 12. Locked out or blocked

### Too many wrong passwords

The bank counts wrong passwords, both on the **Sign in** page and wherever it asks for your password
again (sending money, showing a card number, paying a loan, changing your password).

1. After several wrong passwords in a row, you are locked out for a short time. You see **Too many wrong
   passwords. Try again in … minutes.** On the **Sign in** page, a countdown shows when you can try
   again.
2. If you get it wrong again after that, you are locked out for a little longer.
3. If you get it wrong once more, your login is blocked, you are signed out everywhere, and you see
   **Your account is locked. Please contact your branch.**

Entering the right password resets the count. The count also clears by itself after a while without any
wrong passwords. The bank sets the exact number of attempts and how long each lock lasts.

### Getting back in after a block

A blocked login can only be reopened by the bank. Contact your branch, by visiting or by phone:

1. Branch staff check your identity: your date of birth, your registered phone number and your account
   history.
2. They send an access request to an administrator. You get an SMS telling you the request was
   received.
3. When the administrator approves it, you get one of these SMS messages:
   - **Your account has been unblocked**: sign in with your usual password.
   - **Your temporary password is …**: sign in with that temporary password. You will be asked to choose
     a new password straight away. Nobody at the bank sees the temporary password.
4. If the request is rejected, you get an SMS asking you to visit your branch.

A temporary password only works for a limited time, which the bank sets. If you see **This temporary
password has expired. Please contact your branch.**, contact your branch again for a new one.

### Blocked by the bank

Your branch can also block your login, for example if your card or phone was stolen. You then see **Your
account is blocked. Please contact the bank.** Blocking your login does not affect your accounts. Contact
your branch to have it unblocked.

## 13. Frequently asked questions

### I registered, but I can't send money. Why?
Your identity must be verified at your branch and your first account must be opened there. Take your
national ID to your home branch. See [section 1](#what-happens-next-identity-check-and-your-first-account).

### How much can I transfer in a day?
Up to your account's daily transfer limit, which the bank sets for each account type. The **Transfer**
page shows how much is left today. It resets at midnight, Banjul time.

### Can I send money to someone at another bank?
No. Transfers go between Diamond Bank accounts only.

### Who sees my name when someone sends me money?
Only a partly hidden version of your name, so the sender can check they have the right account.

### My card was stolen. What should I do?
Open **Cards** and click **Block card** straight away. You can then request a replacement.

### Can I have two loans?
No, one at a time. You can apply again once your current loan is closed, rejected or cancelled.

### Is there a fee if I pay my loan late?
No penalty fees are charged, but the instalment shows as **Overdue**. Please pay as soon as you can.

### Can I change my phone number or email online?
No. Visit your branch. Staff will update your profile.

### I forgot my password. What do I do?
There is no online password reset. Contact your branch. After checking your identity they can arrange a
temporary password by SMS (see [section 12](#getting-back-in-after-a-block)).

| **Too many login attempts. Try again in … seconds.** | Too many sign-in attempts from your device | Wait, then try again |

| **Your account is blocked. Please contact the bank.** | The bank has blocked your login | Contact your branch |
| **This temporary password has expired. Please contact your branch.** | The temporary password was not used in time | Contact your branch for a new one |
| **You must change your password before continuing.** | You signed in with a temporary password | Choose a new password on **Change your password** |
| **You were logged out after … minutes of inactivity.** | You were signed out for inactivity | Sign in again |
| **Your identity is being verified. Visit (branch) with your national ID.** | KYC is not done yet | Visit your branch with your national ID |
| **Your verification was not approved. Please contact the bank.** | The bank could not verify your identity | Contact the bank |
| **Visit a branch to open your first account.** | You have no account yet | Visit a branch |
| **Enter a 9-digit Gambian mobile number starting with 83, 86 or 87.** | The phone number format is wrong | Enter 9 digits without the country code |
| **You must be at least 18 years old to register.** | Your date of birth is under 18 | Online registration is for adults only |
| **The password is incorrect.** | Wrong password on a transfer, card, loan payment, or as your current password when changing it | Type it again carefully |
| **This account can't receive transfers. Check the number and try again.** | The recipient's account number is wrong or the account can't receive money | Check the number with the recipient |
| **Insufficient funds: this transfer would take the account below its minimum balance.** | Not enough money in the account | Send a smaller amount or add money first |
| **This transfer is over the daily transfer limit …** | You have reached today's limit for this account | Send what is left today, or wait until after midnight |
| **None of your accounts can send money right now. Please contact your branch.** | None of your accounts is active | Contact your branch |

| **Too many quotes. Wait a moment, then change a value to try again.** | You asked for many quotes very quickly | Wait a moment, then change a value |
| **A guarantor can't be yourself: use someone else's phone number.** | The guarantor's phone or email matches yours | Enter your guarantor's own details |
| **The amount has changed. Please review it again.** | The amount due on your loan changed | Check the new amount and pay again |
| **Insufficient funds: this payment would take the account below its minimum balance.** | Not enough money for the loan payment | Choose another account or add money first |
| **This loan is not active.** | The loan is not being repaid (for example, already paid off) | Open the loan to check its status |
| **Another transaction on this account was being processed at the same moment. Nothing was changed; please try again.** | Two payments touched the account at once | Try again |
| **Can't reach the server. Check your connection and try again.** | Your internet connection dropped | Check your connection and try again |
| **Something went wrong. Please try again.** | An unexpected error | Try again. If it keeps happening, contact your branch |
